DELICOUS RECIPES IN JULY
 
 
 
 
BARE BERRIES & CHIA JAM
 
INGREDIENTS
 
+ 2- 3 tablespoons of Bare Blends Organic Chia Seeds
+ 1.5 tablespoons Bare Berries
+ 1 teaspoon vanilla bean powder
+ 4 table spoons of maple syrup/ coconut sugar/ nectar
+ 1 tablespoon coconut oil
+ 3 cups of mixed frozen or fresh berries
 
DIRECTIONS
1. Put berries, syrup/ sugar, coconut oil in a saucepan - stir frequently.
2. Bring to the boil then simmer (may need to add a bit of water).
3. Mash/squish berries with a fork.
4. Add Chia and cook till becomes thick.
5. Add vanilla bean powder, and Bare Berries and simmer for 5 more mins.
6. Remove from the heat and let cool.
7. Then put in a clean glass container and store in the fridge for up.

SMOOTHIE TIP #1

Soak your nuts!
Not only will this create a creamier smoothie and be easier on your blender, soaking also activates nuts! By activating nuts, you are minimising/eliminating the phytic acid in the outer layer. Untreated phytic acid can combine with calcium, magnesium, copper, iron and especially zinc in the intestinal tract and block their absorption.
